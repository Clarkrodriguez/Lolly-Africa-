FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Browse the products, add items to your cart, then click "Checkout on WhatsApp". Fill in your name, phone and delivery address — your order is sent directly to us on WhatsApp.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer, USSD and cash on delivery. Payment details are confirmed once you reach out on WhatsApp.

How long does delivery take?

Same-day in Lagos for orders placed before 1pm. Outside Lagos, delivery is arranged based on your location.

Are your products fresh?

Yes. Fresh produce is sourced daily from local markets and dispatched the same day to keep things crisp and fresh.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes — simply reply on the same WhatsApp chat before your order leaves for delivery and we'll adjust it.

Do you have a minimum order?

Yes, the minimum order is ₦5,000. Orders above ₦20,000 enjoy free delivery within Lagos.
